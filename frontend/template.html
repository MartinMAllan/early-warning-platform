Development of an Intelligent Web-Based Early Warning Platform for Predicting Student Attrition Risks - MSc group project, University of the West of Scotland.

Built on the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (Kuzilek, Hlosta & Zdrahal, 2017), CC BY 4.0. Risk scores are generated by an XGBoost classifier trained for this prototype and are illustrative, not a deployed institutional system.
